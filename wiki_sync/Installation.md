# Installation

Farbound is distributed as a **Modrinth modpack (`.mrpack`)** for **Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge**. At the moment it is available on Modrinth only.

## Recommended: Modrinth App

1. Download and install the [Modrinth App](https://modrinth.com/app) and sign in with your Minecraft account.
2. Search for **Farbound** and click **Install**, or open the [Farbound page](https://modrinth.com/modpack/farbound) and choose **Play** or **Install**.
3. Open the instance settings and set the memory (see below).
4. Launch the pack and follow the quest mod setup (see below).

The launcher installs the right NeoForge and Java version for you. You do not need to install either by hand.

## Other launchers

Launchers that support `.mrpack` files, such as **Prism Launcher**, work as well. Download the `.mrpack` from the Modrinth page and import it. If something behaves differently from the Modrinth App, mention your launcher when you report it.

## RAM

Allocate at least **8 GB** to Minecraft.

Allocated RAM is what you give Minecraft, not what your PC has in total. Leave enough for your system: 16 GB of system RAM is a comfortable base. In the Modrinth App, find it under *Instance settings > Java and memory*.

## First launch

The very first launch takes noticeably longer (mod setup, asset and shader compilation). **This is normal.** Do not close the game while it looks stuck. Later launches are much faster.

## Quest mod setup

The quest book runs on a few mods that are not allowed to ship inside a Modrinth pack (FTB Quests, FTB Teams and their addons). On the first launch, Farbound shows a screen that walks you through getting them:

1. **Download** the required mods with the buttons on that screen. Each button opens the mod's CurseForge page. Keep that page visible, otherwise the countdown pauses. The download starts on its own when the countdown ends.
2. **Move** the downloaded files into the `mods` folder of your instance. The mod folder button opens it for you.
3. **Enable and restart.** Some files are renamed automatically; the enable button switches them on. Then restart Farbound.

Without them, the game still runs, but you have no quest book. If the screen comes back after an update, repeat the three steps.

## Updating

See [[Updating]]. In short: update through your launcher, never mix files from different versions, and back up your world first.

## Adding or removing mods

For private use you can change the instance as you like, but those changes are outside what is tested. Reproduce problems on an unmodified pack before reporting them. Updating the pack may overwrite your changes.
