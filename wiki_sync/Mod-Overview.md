# Mod Overview

Farbound is a curated pack of more than 500 mods and libraries. Every one earns its place by serving the theme (vehicles, home, exploration) or by keeping the pack running. This page gives an overview by category. The full, versioned list is always on the [Modrinth project page](https://modrinth.com/modpack/farbound).

## The core

| Mod | Role |
|---|---|
| **Create** | The mechanical foundation: kinetics, contraptions, logistics |
| **Sable** | Physics engine, required by Aeronautics |
| **Create: Aeronautics** | Physical vessels that fly, sail and roll, the heart of the pack |

Aeronautics, Sable and Create need matching versions. That is why you update the pack as a whole and never swap one of them by hand.

## Flight

Around Aeronautics sits a whole ecosystem: **Create: Simulated** and **Aeroworks** for extra parts, **Create Propulsion** for thrusters and wings, **Create Flight Control** for flight computers, autopilot and navigation, plus automated logistics, placeable wheels, rope and hose connectors, a toolgun, a preflight checklist, wind sound, propeller blur and camera sync. See [[Your First Airship]].

## Sea and land

- **Sea:** Create: Aquatic Ambitions, Create: Submarine, Wakes for water trails.
- **Land:** Steam 'n' Rails and the Railways Navigator for trains, threaded train ticking, tires and tracks for cars and tanks, Simulated Coasters for roller coasters, Create Tweaked Controllers for gamepads, Icy's Better Horses.
- **Space:** Cosmonautics with its Mars and Deep Air addons for rockets and planetary travel.

See [[Vehicles]].

## The Create workshop

The Create addon family is the backbone of the pack. **Create: Connected**, **Ironworks**, **Deco**, **Framed**, **Compressed**, **Encased** and **Copycats+** expand the building side. **Factory Logistics**, **Compatible Storage**, **Vibrant Vaults** and **Hypertube** grow your logistics. **Create: Power Grid**, **Diesel Generators** and **Create: Nuclear** scale your power, **Modern Tech** and **CC: Tweaked** bring computers. **Big Cannons**, **Warnautics**, **Radar**, **Numismatics**, **Western Pump** and more round it out. If it spins, pumps or ticks, there is probably an addon for it here.

## World generation

**Terralith** and **Tectonic** shape vast continents, **Oh The Biomes We've Gone** and **Nature's Spirit** add biomes and villages. **Better Nether** and **Better End** overhaul the other dimensions. **Supplementaries** and **Amendments** polish the vanilla side, **Spelunkery** keeps the underground interesting, and trees fall when you cut them.

## Structures

The **YUNG's Better** series rebuilds vanilla structures, while **Dungeons Arise** (with **Seven Seas**), **Dungeons & Taverns**, the **Epic** series, the **Luki's** series and **Hopo's Better** ruins add destinations across the dimensions. **Lootr** makes structure loot instanced per player. See [[Exploration]].

## Creatures

**Friends & Foes**, **Flowery Mooblooms**, **Wilder Nature**, **Hybrid Birds** and **Hybrid Aquatic** add life to land, sky and sea. **Creeper** and **Enderman Overhaul** give old enemies biome-specific looks, **Enhanced Celestials** brings lunar events, and **Plushie Buddies** turns every creature into a collectible.

## Home: farm, kitchen and bar

**Farmer's Delight** anchors the kitchen, with a family of addons expanding the menu. **Hands-On Delight** lets you cook by hand, **Brewin' And Chewin'** adds fermenting, **Youkai's Feasts** adds new dishes and utensils. The **Let's Do** series brings farm and charm, bakery, brewery, vinery, herbal brews, alpine cheese, a beach party and furniture. **Starcatcher** makes fishing a hobby of its own. Because this is a Create pack, the kitchen automates with **Create Central Kitchen**, **AutoChef's Delight** and **Slice and Dice**. See [[Home and Kitchen]].

## Home: building and hobbies

Furniture from **Handcrafted**, the **Macaw's** series, **Let's Do Furniture** and **Interiors**, timber frames from **Hearth and Timber**, pottery, **Beautify**, **Chipped** for block variety, photography with **Exposure** (and polaroids), instruments with **Immersive Melodies**, sleeping bags and hammocks from **Comforts**, folk witchcraft from **Hexalia**, and an ambient audio stack with **Ambient Sounds** and **Sound Physics Remastered**.

## Survival and comfort

**Sophisticated Backpacks** and **Sophisticated Storage** (with storage in motion), paragliders, hammers that mine 3x3, vein mining, seats on stairs and slabs, **Keep Some Inventory** and **Extreme Sound Muffler**.

## Exploration and navigation

**Antique Atlas** (the map as an item), **Explorer's Compass**, **Nature's Compass**, **Traveler's Titles** and **Spyglass Improvements**.

## Multiplayer

**Simple Voice Chat** for proximity voice, **FTB Teams** for shared quest progress, **Pings** for pointing at things, and instanced loot so nobody fights over chests. See [[Multiplayer]].

## Quests and progression

**FTB Quests** with extensions (downloaded on first launch, see [[Installation]]), plus **KubeJS** and **CraftTweaker** for recipe integration across the pack.

## Made for Farbound

A few small mods are written for the pack by its author: **Duct Tape**, **Fanfare**, **Greased Gears**, **Tab Tailor** and **Turncoat**.

## Performance and polish

A full optimization stack: **Sodium**, **Lithium**, **ModernFix**, **C2ME**, **FerriteCore**, **EntityCulling**, **ImmediatelyFast** and more, plus **Iris** for shader support (with caveats, see [[Troubleshooting]]) and quality-of-life mods like **EMI** (recipe browser) and **Jade** (block info). See [[Performance and Settings]].
