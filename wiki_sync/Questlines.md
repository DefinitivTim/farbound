# Questlines

Farbound's quest book is written as a travel journal and a gentle guide, not a checklist. It has well over a thousand quests in six groups. Chapters unlock naturally, most side content is open from the start, and rewards celebrate milestones instead of gating them.

> The quest book needs a few extra mods that you download on the first launch, see [[Installation]]. It keeps growing with updates, so your version may differ slightly from this page.

## The journey

| Chapter | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Where it Begins** | The Overworld: first nights, first iron, the first trip underground. Everything starts here. |
| **Little Helpers** | The house rules of Farbound and the small tricks you might otherwise miss. Read it once. |
| **Through the Flames** | The Nether, reshaped: fortresses and bastions, new biomes, nether metals and a kitchen that runs hot. |
| **Edge of the World** | The End: from the stronghold to the outer islands, the dragon, the End cities, alien forests and the rarest metals. |

## Hall of Fame

A trophy for every chapter you complete. Optional quests do not count, so you can finish a chapter without doing everything in it.

## How quests work

- **Tasks** are things you craft, find, tame, photograph or defeat. Vanilla advancements are disabled, quests replace them.
- **Dependencies** keep chapters coherent, but side chapters are open from the start.
- **Rewards** are experience plus one to three items that fit the chapter. Milestones and hard quests add a random reward draw.
- **Plushies** exist only as quest rewards. Collect them all, your airship deserves a plushie shelf.
- **Teams** share quest progress via FTB Teams, see [[Multiplayer]].
