# Vehicles

Vehicles are the heart of Farbound. In this pack anything you build can become a real, physical vessel: it has weight, it tilts, it drifts in the wind, and you can walk around on it while it moves. Airships, boats, cars and rockets all start the same way, with the Physics Assembler.

This page gives an overview. For the step-by-step path, follow the quest chapters named below, see [[Questlines]]. Brand new to flying? Start with [[Your First Airship]].

## Air

**Create: Aeronautics**, running on the **Sable** physics engine, lets you build airships and flying machines that stay fully walkable in flight. Your deck is your base.

Chapter: **Skyward**, in two halves:

- **The basics of flight:** balloons and hot air burners, propellers and engines, steering wheels and throttle levers, sails, sensors for a redstone autopilot, mooring ropes and docking connectors.
- **Ground Control:** the shipyard. Control desks with yokes, joysticks and pedals, turpentine and ion thrusters, wings, flight computers with altitude and attitude hold, recorded routes, airship stations and whole fleets on one logistics screen.

Tips:

- **Start small.** A compact, well balanced ship teaches you more than a huge one.
- **Read the Contraption Diagram** before every maiden voyage. It shows mass, center of mass, lift and thrust, and warns you about missing parts.
- **Elegant beats enormous.** Physics vessels cost real CPU time, see [[Performance and Settings]].
- **Shaders and moving vessels do not always get along**, see [[Troubleshooting]].

## Sea

Rowing boats leave real wakes. Ships get rudders, boat engines, anchors and buoys. Submarines dive with ballast tanks, and a handheld Seaglide pulls you through the water when you leave the hull.

Chapter: **Anchors Aweigh**, which also holds the fishing quests, see [[Home and Kitchen]].

## Land

- **Trains:** stations, signals, schedules and a navigator that plans routes across your network.
- **Cars and trucks:** wheel mounts and tires up to monstrous sizes, sticky tires that grip slopes, caterpillar tracks and tunnel boring machines.
- **Roller coasters:** real physics coasters with launches and loops.
- **Horses:** breeds with their own strengths, gaits from walk to gallop, armor, carts, plows and seeders.

Chapter: **Overland**.

## Space

Late in the pack, mine titanium, build a multi-stage rocket and leave the planet. The Moon and Mars are waiting, and a base up there is a project of its own.

Chapter: **Final Frontier**.

## Weapons

Cannons, autocannons, rockets, bombs and radar for warships and airships. Big cannons are cast from molten metal, from cast iron up to nethersteel. Whether you use them is up to you, and on servers the server's rules come first.

Chapter: **Broadside**.

## Fuel and logistics

- A fuel pump with a hose fills tanks and vehicles, and the hose stays attached to flying vessels as they move.
- Hose connectors carry fluids, items and power between separate vessels, for mid-air refueling or a cargo barge.
- Backpacks and storage that travel along on your vessels are covered in **No More Chests**.

## Controls and comfort

- Gamepads with analog sticks and triggers work through the tweaked controllers.
- Seats can be bolted onto your vessels, so friends can ride along.
- Nameplates give every ship its name. Every proper ship has one.

## Building and server etiquette

If you play on a server, keep an eye on the server's rules for vessels, parking and chunk loaders. Large or numerous vessels cost everyone performance, see [[Multiplayer]].
