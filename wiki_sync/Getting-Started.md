# Getting Started

So you spawned in, the sun is rising, and somewhere out there is a horizon with your name on it. Here is how your first hours in Farbound usually go.

## 0. Get the quest book

On your first launch, the pack asks you to download the quest mods. Do it, it takes two minutes and the quest book is your guide for everything below. See [[Installation]].

## 1. Open the quest book

The quest book is your travel journal. Start with **Where it Begins**: first nights, first iron, the first trip underground. Then open **Little Helpers**, which collects the house rules and handy tricks of the pack. Quests reward you as you go, but they never force a path. See [[Questlines]] for all chapters.

## 2. Settle in

Before any machine turns, you want the basics: tools, food, a roof. Farbound's cozy side is a core of the pack. Farming, cooking, baking and brewing are crafts of their own, and once your machines run, even the kitchen can be automated. Start with **Green Acres** for the fields and **Kitchen Confidential** for the stove. See [[Home and Kitchen]].

## 3. First gears

Your path to a vehicle starts with **Create** and a humble block of andesite. **Clockwork** guides you through rotation and stress, the Andesite Age and the Brass Age. Take your time here: everything you build later stands on it. The engineering path keeps going long after your first flight: steel and casting in **Big Iron**, electricity, diesel and the atom in **Megawatt**, computers in **Bleeding Edge**.

## 4. Build something that moves

In Farbound anything you build can become a real, physical vessel: it has weight, it tilts, it drifts in the wind, and you can walk around on it while it moves. Airships, boats, cars and rockets all start the same way.

- **Air:** your first airship in **Skyward**. The step-by-step version is [[Your First Airship]].
- **Sea:** boats, ships and submarines in **Anchors Aweigh**.
- **Land:** trains, cars, tracked vehicles and horses in **Overland**.
- **Space:** rockets to the Moon and Mars in **Final Frontier**.

Start small. A first flight with three propellers and a lot of luck is a tradition. More on [[Vehicles]].

## 5. Go somewhere

Villages and inns, ruins and dungeons, shipwrecks and pillager airships are waiting. A paraglider turns every cliff into a shortcut, maps and compasses help you find your way, and a camera lets you bring memories home. Loot in generated structures is instanced per player, so nobody can loot a dungeon empty before you get there. See [[Exploration]].

## Pack rules worth knowing

- **No elytra.** Farbound is about vehicles you build. Wings would skip the whole point. Paragliders are allowed.
- **Vanilla advancements are disabled.** The quest book replaces them.
- **Death is forgiving, not free.** You keep most of what you carry. The contents of your backpacks spill onto the ground and wait there for ten minutes.
- **Nights take time.** Sleeping fast-forwards the night instead of skipping it.

More in the [[FAQ]].
