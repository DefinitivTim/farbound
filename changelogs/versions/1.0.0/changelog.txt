First official release of Farbound for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).

- Vehicles of every kind: walkable airships with Create: Aeronautics, steamships and submarines, trains and land vehicles, and rockets late in the pack
- A world worth crossing: vast continents, hand-crafted structures in every dimension, living oceans, loot instanced per player
- A large, curated collection of Create addons: logistics, diesel, radar, cannons, electronics, nuclear power
- A home worth returning to: cooking, baking, brewing, a tavern, a cheese cellar, furniture, photography, instruments
- A quest book as a travel journal, replacing the vanilla advancements
- A full optimization stack; heavy extras like Distant Horizons are optional and ship disabled
- Known issue: Iris shaders can cause rendering problems on moving airships
