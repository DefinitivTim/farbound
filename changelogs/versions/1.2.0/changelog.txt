Important: Several mods were removed in this update, among them Construction Wand and Scribble. Their items will disappear from existing worlds. Please back up your worlds before updating.

Ahoy, Farbounders! Thirty-three new quests, more Create in the kitchen and the workshop, and a download that is half the size. Time to set sail and come home with a story or two.

SETTING SAIL

- 33 new quests. New stops for your journey, from the kitchen to the shipyard. Seven quests of the retired organ questline are gone.
- Ratatouille. More Create-style cooking for the home kitchen.
- Create: Extra Casing and Simulated Copycats. More blocks to build vessels and bases with.
- Create Aeronautics updates. Toolgun 0.3.8, Transmission & Linkage 0.2.9 and Physics Gantry 1.1.1.
- Punchy 3.1 and Punchy Moves. New camera options for ladders, fences, elytra and bows, off by default.
- Trash Cans and Copper Overthrow. Two new additions for your base.
- Half the download. The pack shrank from about 82 MB to 41 MB.
- Longer days. A day now lasts 30 minutes.

NEW ARRIVALS

- Ratatouille, Create: Extra Casing, Aeronautics: Simulated Copycats, Create More: Parallel Pipes and Create: Advanced Optimization
- Punchy Moves, Trash Cans, Copper Overthrow, Scholar and Offgrid
- Bobber Detector, OptiCore, Flerovium, ThreatenGL and fuck Sable

UNDER THE HOOD

- Sophisticated Storage jumped from 1.6.10 to 1.6.16 and Sophisticated Core from 1.5.19 to 1.5.28.
- Reworked quest chapters: Anchors Aweigh, Clockwork, Grand Designs, Kitchen Confidential, More Chests Moooore, Off Duty, Skyward, Survival Kit and Where It Begins.
- Slimmed down the default files, which cut most of the download size.
- Removed the FancyMenu changelog text.

FAREWELLS

- Async, Animated Hearts, Construction Wand and Scribble
- Not Enough Animations with its EMF compat, Effect Descriptions and Sophisticated GUI

CONFIG CHANGES

Server
- Better Days: day length is set in minutes now, 30 per day (was a 20 minute ratio).
- Collective: commands from Serilum mods ask for confirmation first.
- Amendments: held containers can be picked up with the offhand, with a pickup sound and extra particles.
- Item Obliterator: the Energy and Ultimate Trash Cans are blocked.
- Submarine hull: added Copper Overthrow lanterns.

Client defaults
- Flywheel: light smoothness is Tri-Linear (was Smooth).
- Sodium Dynamic Lights: block entities no longer glow.
- Pipes n Physics: fluid surface resolution is 16 (was 64) and the Flywheel tank visual is off.
- Subtle Effects: some effects only appear in matching habitat biomes.
- Dungeon Config: the title screen button is off.
- Particle Rain: new config layout, density settings were renamed.

Resource packs
- Added: Fluid & Foolish 1.0.1 and HyperPunchy 2.8.
- Removed: Bray's Zombie Overhaul, Drodi's Blazes, Drodi's Illagers and the Fresh Animations player pack.
