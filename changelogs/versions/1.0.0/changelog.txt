First official release of Farbound for Minecraft
  1.21.1 on NeoForge.

HIGHLIGHTS
- Vehicles of every kind: walkable airships with
  Create: Aeronautics, steamships and submarines,
  trains and land vehicles, and late in the pack,
  rockets.
- A world worth crossing: vast continents, hand-
  crafted structures in every dimension and living
  oceans. Structure loot is instanced per player.
- Engineering without a wall: a large, curated
  collection of Create addons covering logistics,
  diesel, radar, cannons, electronics and nuclear
  power.
- A home worth returning to: cooking, baking,
  brewing, a tavern, a cheese cellar, furniture,
  photography and instruments, all automatable
  with Create.
- Quests as a travel journal: a large quest book
  that guides without gating. Vanilla advancements
  are replaced by quests that fit the pack.
- Built to run: a full optimization stack. Heavy
  extras like Distant Horizons are optional and
  ship disabled.

PACK DETAILS
- Create, Create: Aeronautics and Sable as the
  technical core.
- Custom item stack groups for REMI, built by
  hand.
- Custom main menu with version check and RAM
  notice.
- Client and server support.
- Recommended: 8 GB RAM (12 GB with Distant
  Horizons).

KNOWN ISSUES
- Shaders (Iris) can cause rendering problems on
  moving airships. See [[Troubleshooting]].
