# Home and Kitchen

Exploring is only half of Farbound. The other half smells like fresh bread. This page covers everything that makes your base a home: farming, cooking, baking, brewing, fishing, furniture and the hobbies for slow days.

All of it lives in the **Hearth and Home** group of the quest book, and those chapters are open from the start. See [[Questlines]].

## Farming

Chapter: **Green Acres**.

- **Wild crops** grow all over the world: cabbages by the sea, tomatoes in warm lands, wild rice in the water. Find them, then plant your own.
- **Right-click to harvest.** Ripe crops are harvested and replanted in one go.
- **Rice** grows in shallow paddies, one block deep.
- **Compost and Rich Soil** make crops grow faster, and mushrooms spread on it.
- **Straw** from the knife turns into rope, canvas and bales.
- Later, head up into the mountains for cattle, cheese and alpine winter evenings.

## Cooking

Chapter: **Kitchen Confidential**.

- The **knife** is your main tool. On the **Cutting Board** it slices, strips and cracks ingredients.
- The **Stove** heats everything above it. The **Cooking Pot** turns up to six ingredients into stews, soups and pastas. The **Skillet** fries, and is a surprisingly good weapon.
- **Cook with your hands.** Ingredients fly into an open pot and you stir by hand: wide, steady rounds earn stars, and every star adds a serving. On the cutting board you cut along the marks, and sandwiches are built layer by layer.
- **Automation:** once your Create workshop runs, machines can chop, mix, bake and cook for you.

## Bakery, cafe and bar

- **Cafe Corner:** dough, bread, croissants, cakes, cookies and jam, with tea, coffee and herbal brews on the side. The cozy, alcohol-free corner.
- **Happy Hour:** beer from your own hop garden, whiskey from copper stills, wine from your vineyard, and barrels to age it all. Ends in a festival hall worth sharing.

## Fishing

Chapters: **Anchors Aweigh** and the kitchen chapters.

- The seas hold dozens of fish and stranger things of the deep. Many only bite in certain waters, at certain times or in certain weather.
- A **fishing guide** remembers every fish you have caught, and where and when it bites.
- **Bait matters.** Bone meal on farmland now and then turns up worms, and special bait lures particular fish.
- On the cutting board a fish becomes a fillet that **keeps its rarity**. A rare catch makes a better dish.
- Catch every kind at least once, and the waters hold one last legendary prize.

## Furnishing your home

Chapter: **Grand Design**.

- Furniture of every style: couches, beds, kitchens, wardrobes, bookshelves, trophies of the creatures you have met.
- The **Hammer** changes the look of placed furniture: cushions, sheets and variants.
- Lights, roofs, windows, doors, fences and paths in many materials, timber frames and pottery.
- Industrial chic from the Create deco family, and thousands of block variants for builders.
- **Sit anywhere.** Press X on stairs and slabs to sit down.

## Hobbies

- **Off Duty:** a beach hut with a bar and cocktails, **photography** with a working camera, film and polaroids, and **music** on instruments you play by hand, alone or together.
- **Hedge Witchery:** herbs, small cauldrons, rituals and witch brews, like one that takes you home.

## Small comforts

- A **Sleeping Bag** lets you sleep anywhere without moving your spawn point. A **Hammock** is for daytime naps.
- The **sound muffler** turns down any noise that wears thin, so your base stays calm.
- **Backpacks** and smarter storage grow with you, see **Backpacker** and **No More Chests**.
