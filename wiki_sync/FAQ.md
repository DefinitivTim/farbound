# FAQ

## General

**What Minecraft version and loader does Farbound use?**
Minecraft **1.21.1** on **NeoForge**. Fabric, Forge and Bedrock are not supported.

**Where can I download it?**
On [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/modpack/farbound). There is no other official download.

**Is the pack released?**
Farbound was first released on 9 October 2026 and is available on Modrinth. The pack keeps receiving updates, and the quest book keeps growing. Back up your worlds before updating.

**Is it only about airships?**
No. Airships are the heart, but boats, submarines, trains, cars and rockets are just as welcome, and a cozy home is a core of the pack. See [[Vehicles]] and [[Home and Kitchen]].

**Where do I report bugs or give feedback?**
On the [issue tracker](https://github.com/DefinitivTim/farbound/issues/new/choose). For performance issues, please include specs, allocated RAM and whether shaders were active.

## Gameplay

**Where is my elytra?**
Gone, and on purpose. Farbound is about vehicles you build yourself. Strapping on wings would skip the entire journey. A paraglider gets you off cliffs, and an airship is roomier anyway. See [[Your First Airship]].

**Where is the quest book?**
The quest mods are downloaded on the first launch. If you skipped that step, see [[Installation]].

**Why don't I get advancements?**
Vanilla advancements are disabled. The quest book replaces them with quests that fit the pack, including reworked versions of the classic milestones.

**I died. Where is my stuff?**
You keep most of what you carry. The contents of your backpacks spill onto the ground where you fell and wait there for ten minutes, so hurry back.

**Someone looted the dungeon before me!**
They did not. Structure loot is instanced per player (Lootr). Everyone opens their own version of each chest.

**Do quests work in multiplayer?**
Yes. Quest progress is shared within your team via FTB Teams. See [[Multiplayer]].

**Does the pack have voice chat?**
Yes, Simple Voice Chat is included. On servers with the matching server mod you get proximity voice.

**What are these plushies?**
Collectible rewards. Meeting, taming or defeating the world's creatures earns you their plushie counterparts. Yes, you can collect them all. Yes, your airship deserves a plushie shelf.

## Technical

**The first launch takes forever. Is something broken?**
No. The first launch sets up mods and compiles assets, which takes a while. Later launches are much faster.

**My airship looks broken with shaders.**
Known issue: Create: Aeronautics has rendering problems with Iris shaders on moving vessels. Try without shaders. See [[Troubleshooting]].

**Is Distant Horizons included?**
No, not anymore. It was removed from the pack, so the RAM recommendation is 8 GB for everyone.

**How do I update without losing my world?**
Back up, read the changelog, update through your launcher. Details on [[Updating]].

**Can I add or remove mods?**
For your own private use, sure. Added mods are outside what is tested, so please reproduce bugs on an unmodified pack before reporting them. Updates may overwrite your changes.

**Can I use the pack on my server?**
Yes, server use is explicitly allowed. See [[License and Credits]] for what is and is not permitted.

**Can I put Farbound in my own modpack, fork it or upload it elsewhere?**
No, not without written permission. See [[License and Credits]].
