# Exploration

Nothing in Farbound forces you out of your base. But there is always something on the horizon worth the trip, and the best expeditions come home with loot and stories. This page covers the world, what you can find in it and how to find your way.

Quest chapters: **Wanderlust** for the friendly side of the world, **Treasure Hunters** for the dangerous side. See [[Questlines]].

## The world

- **Vast continents** with dramatic mountains, deep valleys and long coastlines.
- **Many more biomes**, from giant sequoia forests and cherry groves to canyons, geysers and mushroom fields.
- **Living oceans** full of fish, whales and reefs.
- **Moods in the night sky.** Some moons light up the depths, some calm the world, and a Blood Moon does the opposite. Check the sky before you plan the night.

## Places to find

| Friendly | Dangerous |
|---|---|
| Villages in every style, towns and castles | Ruins, dungeons and crypts |
| Inns and taverns, lighthouses and watchtowers | Temples, mansions and illager citadels |
| Wizard towers and herbalist huts | Ancient cities and the deep dark |
| Wonders of nature to photograph | Shipwrecks, sunken cities and ocean monuments |
| | Pillager airships patrolling the sky |

Loot in generated structures is **instanced per player**. Everyone opens their own version of each chest, so nobody can loot a dungeon empty before you arrive.

## Finding your way

- **Atlas:** a map that grows as you travel and marks what you have seen.
- **Explorer's Compass** points to a structure of your choice, **Nature's Compass** to a biome.
- **Spyglass** improvements make scouting from the deck easier.
- **Title cards** greet you whenever you enter a new biome or dimension.
- On your airship, a **Navigation Table** turns compasses and maps into redstone signals, so the ship can point itself home. See [[Your First Airship]].

## Getting around

- **Paragliders** turn every cliff into a shortcut. Upgrade them from paper to netherite.
- **Vessels** are the real way to travel. See [[Vehicles]].
- **Horses** are the first vehicle without a single gear.
- **No elytra.** The sky is crossed by ships you build.

## Other worlds

- **Through the Flames:** the Nether, reshaped, with new biomes, fortresses, bastions and nether metals.
- **Edge of the World:** the End, from the stronghold to the outer islands, alien forests and the rarest metals.
- **Final Frontier:** rockets to the Moon and Mars, late in the pack.

## Expedition tips

- Pack a **Sleeping Bag**. It lets you sleep without moving your spawn point.
- Take food that keeps. A good meal is half the expedition.
- **Death is forgiving.** You keep most of what you carry, but the contents of your backpacks drop and wait for ten minutes. Do not wander too far before going back.
- Bring a **camera** and fill your album. Hang the best pictures at home.
