# Welcome to the Farbound Wiki

> *Build your vessel. Chart the world. And always leave a light on at home.*

**Farbound** is a cozy engineering adventure for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**, built around **Create** and **Create: Aeronautics**. Build airships, boats, submarines, trains and land vehicles, take them out into a world that rewards the curious, and come home to a warm kitchen, a workshop full of gears and a campfire under the stars.

🎉 **Farbound is out on Modrinth.** The pack keeps receiving updates. Back up your worlds before you update.

## What makes Farbound different

Most Create packs live on the ground: conveyor belts, factories, automation. Farbound shifts the focus to **movement, exploration and home**. Your vessel is workshop, base and transport in one, whether it flies, sails or rolls. The world is generated with vast continents, striking structures and living oceans, and the quest book accompanies your journey without ever forcing your hand. And when you come back, there is bread in the oven.

## Design pillars

1. **The vehicle is the hero.** Airships, watercraft and land vehicles. Every feature earns its place by making building, steering or using a vessel more interesting.
2. **A home worth returning to.** Farming, cooking, brewing and decorating are a core of the pack, not decoration.
3. **Exploration is rewarded, never forced.** You set out because something waits over the horizon, not because a quest demands it.
4. **Progression is earned, not grindy.** From your first wobbly balloon to a real airship, step by step.
5. **It has to run well.** Performance and stability are a feature, not an afterthought.
